I created a function named calculateShelterCoverage() that accepts an array of water counts and a daily water requirement. The function validates the daily requirement and checks every shelter's water stock. It rejects invalid values such as negative numbers, non-integers, and stocks greater than 1,000,000. I used a for loop to validate the water counts and a for...of loop to calculate the complete days of coverage and total water supply. I also used an array named coverage to store the complete days of water for each shelter..
